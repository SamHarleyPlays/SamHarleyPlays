Licensing

One licence costs US$3.50 and lets one group stage one play for 12 months. Buying the licence gets you the full script as a PDF.

What the licence covers

  • One group: a theatre company, school, drama club or festival entrant.
  • Any number of performances within 12 months of purchase, including festival heats, finals and encore nights.
  • Printing as many copies of the script as your cast and crew need.
  • Filming for festival judging, your own archive, and short non-commercial clips on social media.

What we ask in return

  • Credit the play in your programme and publicity as: [Title] by Sam Harley.
  • Perform the text as written. Changing local references (a town, a shop name) to suit your audience is fine, and so are the character swaps noted in each script. Anything beyond that, please ask first.
  • Each group needs its own licence. If another group wants to stage the play, they can license it here.

What needs separate permission

  • Commercial recordings, broadcast or streaming of a full performance.
  • Publishing the script in an anthology or collection.
  • Adapting the play into another form, such as a film or a longer play.

About Sam Harley

Sam Harley writes short plays for the stage: comedies built for small casts, simple sets and festival time limits, each with a turn at the end that the audience doesn't see coming.

The plays are written to be practical. Most need two or three actors, a table and a couple of chairs, and at most one sound effect, so a group can put one on with a few weeks of rehearsal and very little budget.

New plays are added regularly. To ask about a play, a festival or a licence, email hello@samharleyplays.com (hello@samharleyplays.com).
